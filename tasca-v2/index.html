Answers

Good questions.

01How long is the free trial?

14 days, and no card is needed. Sign up in your browser and run your own team's work through it. After the trial, write to hello@talbotiq.com.

02Do I need a card to start?

No. The trial needs no card.

03What does it cost after the trial?

We do not publish pricing. After your trial, contact hello@talbotiq.com.

04Who can do what in my workspace?

tasca has five roles: Owner, Admin, Manager, Member and Viewer. On a task, only its creator, its assignee or a manager can change it.

05Can I run more than one workspace?

Yes. You switch between workspaces from the sidebar, and each workspace is kept separate.

06Does Talxa send my text to an AI provider?

Yes. tasca's AI features send text to an AI model provider, and the answers depend on that provider. For more detail, write to hello@talbotiq.com.

07Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. A mobile app is marked "coming soon". Today tasca runs in your browser.

08What is Talxa?

Talxa is the AI agent built into tasca. Ask it "What should I focus on today?" and it looks up your tasks and tells you what is overdue or on hold, who owns it, and which project is off track.

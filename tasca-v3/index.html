Talxa · step by step

How Talxa works.

The mechanism, one step at a time.

  1. Prompt

    You ask

    Type a question in the Talxa panel, for example “What should I focus on today?”

  2. Looked up tasks

    It looks up your work

    Talxa reads your live tasks, owners and priorities. The chat shows the tool call as “Looked up tasks”.

  3. Answer

    It answers from live data

    The reply lists what is overdue or on hold, who owns it, its priority, and which project is off track.

  4. Permission

    It asks first

    Talxa always asks first before destructive actions.

Workspace

The workspace Talxa works on.

Tasks, projects, planning, approvals, meetings and notes live in one workspace. Talxa answers from that live data.

FAQ

Before you start.

01Does Talxa send my text to an AI provider?

Yes. tasca's AI features send text to an AI model provider, and the answers depend on that provider. For more detail, write to hello@talbotiq.com.

02What is Talxa?

Talxa is the AI agent built into tasca. Ask it “What should I focus on today?” and it looks up your tasks and tells you what is overdue or on hold, who owns it, and which project is off track.

03How long is the free trial?

tasca has a 14-day free trial.

04Do I need a card to start?

No. The trial needs no card.

05What does it cost after the trial?

We do not publish pricing. After your trial, contact hello@talbotiq.com.

06Can I run more than one workspace?

Yes. You switch between workspaces from the sidebar, and each workspace is kept separate.

07Who can do what in my workspace?

tasca has five roles: Owner, Admin, Manager, Member and Viewer. On a task, only its creator, its assignee or a manager can change it.

08Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. A mobile app is marked “coming soon”. Today tasca runs in your browser.

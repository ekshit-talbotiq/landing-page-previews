Approvals queue

Quotations, sales orders and invoices waiting for approval land in a single Pending Approvals queue, opened from any page.

The path

The path a deal takes.

From opportunity to receivables, in the order the work happens.

  1. Deal

    Opportunity

    Move the deal through eight stages: New, Qualified, Negotiation, Quotation, Won, Invoiced, Closed and Lost. Expected revenue and costing show gross profit and margin on the deal itself.

  2. Quote

    Quotation

    Build the quotation on the deal, with items, discounts, tax, charges and terms. Its status runs from Draft to Approved to Completed, and it can be viewed as an A4 page.

  3. Decision

    Approval

    Quotations, sales orders and invoices waiting for approval appear in one Pending Approvals queue, opened from a header button on any page.

  4. Order

    Sales order

    An approved quotation is converted into a sales order that links back to its source quotation. The order shows Partially Invoiced or Invoice Created.

  5. Billing

    Invoice

    Invoice the order in full or in progress slices. Each invoice shows its total and what is still outstanding.

  6. Balance

    Receivables

    AR Ageing shows each customer's unpaid balance by days overdue. A statement of account prints for any customer and period.

FAQ

Plain answers.

  1. 01

    What is quote to cash?

    It is the path a sale takes from the first conversation to money collected: opportunity, quotation, sales order, invoice and payment. NousCRM keeps those steps on one deal and shows receivables next to the pipeline.

  2. 02

    How do approvals work?

    Quotations, sales orders and invoices that are waiting for approval appear in one Pending Approvals queue, with a count for each type. A button in the header opens it from any page, and it shows “All caught up” when nothing is waiting.

  3. 03

    What is progress billing?

    Instead of invoicing a whole order at once, you bill it in slices. The sales order shows Partially Invoiced until the rest is billed, and each invoice shows its total and what is still outstanding.

  4. 04

    What do AR ageing and customer statements show?

    AR Ageing lists each customer's unpaid balance as not yet overdue, up to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and 91+ days, with a total unpaid, an as-of date and CSV export. Customer statements show a statement of account per customer and period, with print and CSV export.

  5. 05

    Can I run more than one workspace, and are there roles?

    Yes. There is a workspace switcher and a New workspace option, and each account has a role. Document numbers use a prefix per workspace. We do not publish a roles and permissions matrix here, so ask us for the detail.

  6. 06

    Is there a dark mode?

    Yes. The whole interface is available in light and dark, and a Ctrl K command bar lets you search records, jump to a page or run an action.

  7. 07

    Does NousCRM include AI?

    The interface includes an assistant panel called Noah. We make no claims here about what Noah can do; ask us about its current status when you get in touch.

  8. 08

    What does it cost, and is there a free trial?

    Pricing is not listed on this page and we do not advertise a free trial. Ask us about pricing and terms when you get in touch.

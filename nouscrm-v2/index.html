FAQ

Short answers.

01What is quote to cash?

It is the path a sale takes from the first conversation to money in: opportunity, quotation, sales order, invoice and payment. NousCRM keeps those steps on one deal and shows receivables next to the pipeline.

02How do approvals work?

Quotations, sales orders and invoices that are waiting for approval appear in one Pending Approvals queue, with a count for each type. A button in the header opens it from any page, and it shows "All caught up" when nothing is waiting.

03What is progress billing?

Instead of invoicing a whole order at once, you bill it in slices. The sales order shows Partially Invoiced until the rest is billed, and each invoice shows its total and what is still outstanding.

04What do AR ageing and customer statements show?

AR Ageing lists each customer's unpaid balance as not yet overdue, up to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and 91+ days, with a total unpaid, an as-of date and CSV export. Customer statements show a statement of account per customer and period, with print and CSV export.

05Can I run more than one workspace, and are there roles?

Yes. There is a workspace switcher and a New workspace option, and each account has a role. Document numbers use a prefix per workspace. We do not publish a roles and permissions matrix here, so ask us for the detail.

06Is there a dark mode?

Yes. The whole interface is available in light and dark, and a Ctrl K command bar lets you search records, jump to a page or run an action.

07Does NousCRM include AI?

The interface includes an assistant panel called Noah. We make no claims here about what Noah can do; ask us about its current status when you get in touch.

08What does it cost, and is there a free trial?

Pricing is not listed on this page, and we do not advertise a free trial. Ask us about pricing and terms when you get in touch.
